PhotoVault — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-27

PhotoVault (”the app”, ”we”, ”our”) is published by Azura Group. This page describes what data the app handles and how. Plain summary first, detail below.

TL;DR

  • PhotoVault is an on-device app. We don’t operate servers, we don’t have user accounts, and we do not collect, transmit or share any of your data.
  • Photos you choose to put inside the private space — and any contacts or notes you enter manually inside it — are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key generated inside the Android Keystore on your device.
  • The app does not embed advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Data we collect

None. PhotoVault does not collect personal data, telemetry, identifiers, device information, IP addresses, or usage analytics.

Data the app accesses on your device

PhotoVault asks for these Android permissions, only when you use a feature that needs them. None of the data leaves your device.

PermissionWhyWhen it’s asked
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13+) /
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (≤12)		 Display your gallery and let you import photos into the encrypted private space. The first time you open the gallery.
USE_BIOMETRIC (optional) Unlock the private space with a fingerprint or face on devices that support it. Only if you enable biometric unlock.

You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → PhotoVault → Permissions.

How your private data is stored

  • Live storage: encrypted blobs sit in filesDir under your app sandbox. The encryption key is generated and stored inside the Android Keystore, which on most devices is hardware-backed. The key is non-extractable and tied to your app’s UID.
  • Backups (optional): if you enable “Backup & restore” you choose a folder via the Storage Access Framework and supply a passphrase. The backup is written to that folder encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 250 000 iterations, AES-256-GCM). We never see the passphrase.
  • Sharing: when you tap “Share” on a photo inside the private space, the app decrypts a copy to a private cache folder and hands a content:// URI to the system share sheet. The cache is wiped after the share. We do not see what you share.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information about children — and as noted above, we don’t collect information about anyone.

Changes to this policy

If material aspects of how the app handles data ever change, we will update this page and bump the “Last updated” date above. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
